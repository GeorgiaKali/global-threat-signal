These are articles and research write-ups by <a rel="me" href="https://georgiakali.github.io">Georgia Kali</a> on global threats, security incidents, and risk intelligence.

The writing sits alongside [Global Threat Signal](https://globalthreatsignal.org), a research platform that monitors risks and threats on a world map. The map covers environmental change, geopolitical conflict, technological disruption, public health, economic instability, and large-scale criminal activity.

This site is the place for longer notes. It is not an alert feed, and it is not the map.

The author hub is <a rel="me" href="https://georgiakali.github.io">georgiakali.github.io</a>.
