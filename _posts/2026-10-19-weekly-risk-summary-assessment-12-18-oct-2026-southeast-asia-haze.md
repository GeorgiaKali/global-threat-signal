## Top threat of the week: the Southeast Asian haze crisis

At 8am on 13 October, Singapore's 24-hour Pollutant Standards Index was unhealthy in all five regions, peaking at 173 in the west, as south-easterly winds carried smoke from fires in Kalimantan and southern Sumatra towards the island [[1]](https://globalthreatsignal.org/threat/haze-leaves-air-quality-unhealthy-across-singapore-60350). On 16 October, Singapore's National Environment Agency issued preventive measure notices under the Transboundary Haze Pollution Act to seven companies with concessions in Indonesia [[10]](https://globalthreatsignal.org/threat/singapore-issues-haze-notices-to-seven-indonesian-companies-60607). In Malaysia, 34 of 68 monitoring stations recorded unhealthy air on 15 October and none recorded good air, with schools closed in several areas [[2]](https://globalthreatsignal.org/threat/haze-leaves-34-malaysian-air-monitoring-stations-unhealthy-60505). By 18 October, Foreign Minister Mohamad Hasan said Malaysia was offering helicopters and water-bombing aircraft to Indonesia, warning that visibility had reportedly fallen below 50 metres and Kalimantan's Air Pollutant Index had reached 1,000 [[4]](https://globalthreatsignal.org/threat/malaysia-offers-aircraft-to-help-fight-indonesian-forest-fires-as-haze-worsens-60861). The effects reached Brunei [[11]](https://globalthreatsignal.org/threat/brunei-reports-moderate-air-quality-in-two-districts-60844) and the Maldives, where Malé recorded an AQI of 104 [[3]](https://globalthreatsignal.org/threat/haze-raises-air-quality-concerns-across-mal-and-other-parts-of-maldives-60348).

**Assessment.** The haze has become a multi-country public health and economic disruption rather than a local nuisance. The likelihood that unhealthy air persists across Singapore, Malaysia and neighbouring states over the coming week is judged **high**, and the potential impact **significant**, through school closures and respiratory illness among vulnerable groups. Poor visibility is itself hampering aerial firefighting [[4]](https://globalthreatsignal.org/threat/malaysia-offers-aircraft-to-help-fight-indonesian-forest-fires-as-haze-worsens-60861), and Singapore's notices to Indonesian concession holders add a regulatory and diplomatic dimension [[10]](https://globalthreatsignal.org/threat/singapore-issues-haze-notices-to-seven-indonesian-companies-60607).

## Regional risk assessment

### Middle East and the Gulf

The Antigua and Barbuda-flagged bulk carrier Cape Daw sank after salvage failed, following a projectile strike on 23 September [[5]](https://globalthreatsignal.org/threat/antigua-and-barbuda-flagged-bulk-carrier-sinks-near-strait-of-hormuz-60525). On 18 October, UKMTO reported that a southbound tug was struck by two unknown projectiles north-east of Khor Fakkan in the UAE; one person was slightly injured and no one claimed responsibility [[6]](https://globalthreatsignal.org/threat/tug-struck-by-two-projectiles-northeast-of-khor-fakkan-60885). The likelihood of further attacks on shipping is judged **high** and the impact **severe**. The fiscal strain is visible in Iraq, where the Prime Minister directed a 3.5 trillion dinar (about $2.7 billion) funding package amid pressure from disrupted oil exports and falling revenues [[12]](https://globalthreatsignal.org/threat/iraq-allocates-2-7-billion-to-stimulate-economic-growth-60848). Near Taybeh in the West Bank, masked settlers surrounded diplomats from France, Belgium and Poland and assaulted a French mayor; Israel's foreign minister condemned the attack [[7]](https://globalthreatsignal.org/threat/israeli-settlers-attack-european-diplomats-near-taybeh-60850). Commercial goods stopped entering Gaza for two days amid a tax dispute, raising concern about shortages [[13]](https://globalthreatsignal.org/threat/commercial-goods-halted-from-entering-gaza-amid-tax-dispute-60606), and in Beirut armed men obstructed a police raid on a pharmaceutical warehouse until the army intervened [[14]](https://globalthreatsignal.org/threat/armed-men-obstruct-raid-on-beirut-pharmaceutical-warehouse-60604). Likelihood of further such incidents: **high**; impact: **moderate**, with diplomatic consequences.

In Albania, student-led protests in Tirana turned violent for a third day on 15 October; the Health Ministry reported 89 people injured, including 61 police officers [[17]](https://globalthreatsignal.org/threat/tirana-protests-turn-violent-for-third-consecutive-day-60540). Likelihood of further hybrid incidents: **moderate**; impact: **moderate**.
