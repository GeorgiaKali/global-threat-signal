## What belongs in a note

Use one post for one development. Say what is known, what is still open, and why it matters. Tie claims to sources. Leave out anything you cannot point to.

A usable shape:

1. What happened, with the date and place.
2. What is confirmed, and what is only claimed.
3. Why it matters for the risk, beyond the headline.
4. What would change the assessment.
